Flight manual

Your design is the physics

No stat screens pretending to be engineering. The ship is one rigidbody assembled from your blocks, and everything you feel in flight falls out of where you put them.

01Build like a fort, fly like a brick

Minecraft-style shipyard. Mass and center of gravity come from your blocks; thrust is applied at each engine's mounted position. Hang both engines on one side and the ship genuinely veers — the engineering panel and balance markers tell you before launch.

02Mk I / Mk II everything

Hull, thrusters, RCS pods, guns, armor — each in two marks, where Mk II is better and cooler: blue-burning bells, twin barrels, gyro rings. Armor soaks up to five hits and scorches visibly as it fails.

03You never die

The core is indestructible. Lose every engine and you're stranded, not dead — guns and RCS still answer, so hold the line as a drifting turret or respawn at the carrier with your score intact. People find clever ways home.

04One sky, many worlds

All three planets hang in one continuous space — no loading screens, no map select. Fly into a globe's cloud shell and you're inside its weather; climb back above the clouds and you're in clean zero-g again.

05Turbo, anchor, armor

T burns the engine heat pool for 320 m/s transits. G anchors you dead still against gravity and gunfire — turret mode. F raises armor mode: every hit lands on your plating until they break through it.

06The carrier is home

Launch from a capital ship with blinking beacons and a lit runway. Set down on the pad under 6 m/s and press E to refit mid-sortie — the shipyard opens without leaving the world.

System chart

Three worlds, three storms

Punch through a cloud shell and the game hands you a different scene: flat ground, looping horizon, that world's weather owning the sky.

Korrath

Cloud banks · lightning

A rocky world ringed by an orbital rock belt. Inside: white fog, drifting cloud towers, moss flats and dark ponds — and lightning deep in the deck.

Vessa

Snow storm

The small ice world. Pale skies, flakes streaming past the canopy, frozen lakes glinting between snow mounds. Quiet — until the radar isn't.

Titanhold

Giant dust storm · deadly ring

The Saturn of the system. A four-band golden ring races around it with debris streaming at 2 km/s — inside the band you lose a block twice a second. Below the clouds: sand-gold murk down to wind-scoured flats.

Every surface loops — hold your heading for three kilometers and you arrive exactly where you started. Flat maps that play round: cheap to render, impossible to fall off.

Build from source — the real download
  1. Clone the repo and open it in Unity 2020.3.16f1.
  2. Open an empty scene. Delete everything in it.
  3. Create one empty GameObject named GameManager and add the GameManager script.
  4. Press Play. The menu, carrier, worlds, and everything else generate at runtime.
